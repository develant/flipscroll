Install

Takes about a minute.

  1. Download and unzipMove FlipScroll.app into your Applications folder.
  2. Open itA new icon appears in the menu bar. FlipScroll is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.
  3. Allow Accessibility accessmacOS asks for permission so the app can adjust scroll events. Switch on FlipScroll in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility.
  4. Pick your directionsClick the menu bar icon and tick what to reverse for the mouse and the trackpad.

Questions

Is FlipScroll a scroll reverser?

Yes. FlipScroll is a free scroll reverser for macOS: it reverses the scroll direction of your mouse and, if you want, your trackpad. Unlike most scroll reversers it handles horizontal scrolling separately, so a mouse can scroll the classic way in every direction while the trackpad keeps natural scrolling.

Can I turn off natural scrolling for the mouse only?

macOS can’t do it on its own because one setting covers every device. Keep natural scrolling on in System Settings and let FlipScroll reverse the mouse; the trackpad stays natural.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

Changing scroll direction means adjusting scroll events before apps receive them, and macOS only allows that for apps with Accessibility permission. The app reads nothing else and sends nothing anywhere.

Should natural scrolling be on or off in System Settings?

Leave it on, as most trackpad users do. Then reverse both directions for the mouse and nothing for the trackpad. If you prefer it off, flip the choices the other way.

It stopped working after an update.

macOS sometimes forgets the permission when an app is replaced. In System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility, remove FlipScroll with the – button and add it again.

Does it work with a Magic Mouse?

Yes. FlipScroll checks which device sent each scroll, so a Magic Mouse follows the mouse settings and a trackpad follows the trackpad settings, even though both scroll by touch.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the menu bar, move it from Applications to the Bin, and remove it from the Accessibility list.
